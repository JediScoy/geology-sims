Check your thinking (describe what you saw first)
  • Human wave (stand up, sit down, the wave moves down the row): that is an S-wave motion. Students move up and down. The wave moves sideways.
  • Slinky push and pull: that is a P-wave. The coils squeeze together and spread apart.
  • Turn on the liquid zone. Liquids cannot be sheared, so the S-wave stops. P-waves keep going. This is how scientists learned Earth has a liquid outer core.
  • The gap between the P and S arrival tells you how far away the earthquake was.
